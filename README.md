## 🔬 Research Interests

- **Reasoning in Language Models** — how prompting, decoding, and tool use shape what a model can reliably work out, and how far they substitute for scale.
- **Low-Resource Language Technology** — under-served languages, where data scarcity makes curation and evaluation as consequential as modelling.
- **Robustness & Generalisation** — how models behave under distribution shift, and what benchmark performance does and does not predict.
- **Machine Learning for Security** — learned systems in adversarial settings, and the security of the models and pipelines around them.
- **Systems & Data Engineering** — low-level programming, data modelling, and tooling — the substrate research prototypes are built on.
